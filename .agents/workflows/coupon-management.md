# Admin Coupon Management Workflow

This guide explains how to create, manage, and distribute promotional coupon codes to students.

## 1. Accessing Coupon Management
1. Log in to your **Admin Account**.
2. Click on your profile icon/name in the top right.
3. Select **"Manage Coupons"** from the dropdown menu.
   - Alternatively, navigate directly to `/admin/coupons`.

## 2. Creating a New Coupon
1. Click the **"+ Create New Coupon"** button in the top right.
2. Fill in the following details:
   - **Coupon Code**: The text students will enter (e.g., `WELCOME50`). *Note: Codes are automatically converted to uppercase.*
   - **Discount Type**:
     - `Percentage`: Deducts a percentage of the subtotal (e.g., 20% off).
     - `Fixed Amount`: Deducts a set amount (e.g., ₹50 off).
   - **Value**: The percentage or amount to discount.
   - **Min Order (₹)**: The minimum cart subtotal required to use this coupon.
   - **Max Discount (₹)**: (Only for Percentage type) The maximum discount amount allowed, regardless of cart size.
   - **Expiry Date**: When the coupon will stop working.
   - **Usage Limit**: Total number of times this coupon can be used across all students. Leave blank for unlimited.
3. Click **"Confirm & Create"**.

## 3. Managing Existing Coupons
- **Toggle Status**: Click the green checkmark or gray X icon to activate/deactivate a coupon instantly.
- **Delete**: Click the red trash icon to permanently remove a coupon.
- **Track Usage**: View the "Usage" count on each coupon card to see how many times it has been applied to orders.

## 4. Best Practices for Distribution
- **Targeted Offers**: Create codes like `EXAMFUEL` during finals week.
- **Limited Time**: Set short expiry dates for flash sales to drive immediate orders.
- **Caps**: Always set a `Max Discount` for high-percentage coupons to protect vendor margins.

> [!TIP]
> You can create specific codes for different marketing channels (e.g., `INSTA10`, `FLYER20`) to track which ads are most effective.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Coupons only apply to the **Subtotal**. Taxes, Platform Fees, and Delivery Fees are calculated on the full amount unless the coupon brings the subtotal below the free delivery threshold.
